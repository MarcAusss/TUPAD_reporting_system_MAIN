TUPAD REPORTING SYSTEM
PHASE 12.1 - PSGC MUNICIPALITY NAME COMPATIBILITY HOTFIX

ERROR
-----
CurrentSystemDemoSeeder failed at:

CurrentSystemDemoSeeder.php:349
Municipality::firstOrFail()

after BicolLocationSeeder successfully downloaded and seeded all Bicol
municipalities/cities.

CAUSE
-----
BicolLocationSeeder stores the raw municipality/city name returned by PSGC.

For cities, PSGC can use names such as:

City of Tabaco
City of Legazpi
City of Naga
City of Sorsogon

The demo seeder was searching with common display forms such as:

Tabaco City
Legazpi City
Naga City
Sorsogon City

The records represent the same locations, but an exact WHERE name = ...
comparison cannot match the two forms.

FIX
---
CurrentSystemDemoSeeder now resolves municipalities through a normalized
PSGC-compatible lookup.

Examples treated as equivalent:

City of Tabaco   = Tabaco City
City of Legazpi  = Legazpi City
City of Naga     = Naga City
City of Sorsogon = Sorsogon City

The same resolver is used in BOTH:
1. the project's primary municipality lookup
2. the project's multi-location municipality lookup

The resolver also normalizes accents/punctuation consistently with the
existing BicolLocationSeeder approach.

If a municipality still cannot be resolved, the exception now reports the
requested municipality and all active PSGC names in that province instead of
only throwing ModelNotFoundException.

NO MIGRATION.
NO DATABASE SCHEMA CHANGE.
NO BUSINESS LOGIC CHANGE.
